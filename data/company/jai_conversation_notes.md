Notes from a conversation with Jai (Commercial lead) about how procurement is handled today.

- Jai approves every response before it is sent to a customer. He is the only approver today.
- To check an answer is correct, Jai asks the team that owns it (for example, Engineering for an architecture diagram).
- Recency matters: evidence like a red-team or penetration-test report older than about 6 months will not work for a new customer. Renew it early instead of waiting for the customer to ask.
- Customers send questionnaires in rounds. A later round repeats some earlier questions, sometimes with something added, and question numbers can repeat.
- Answers change during a deal. If we told a customer an audit was in progress and it becomes final, we tell them straight away.
- Answers that come from the compliance platform (Vanta) are trusted more than other sources.
- There is no central record of what has been sent to each customer.
- A typical deal takes 2-3 months.
